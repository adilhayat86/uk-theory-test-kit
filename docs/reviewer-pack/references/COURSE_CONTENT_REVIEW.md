## Authored scope

The Android paid-MVP preparation contains **15 modules and 38 substantive lessons**, supported by a **309-question bank**: the retained 228 questions, 72 new course-gap questions and nine questions linked to three original silent video case studies. This is an independently authored Great Britain car-revision course. It is not the licensed DVSA revision bank, a complete Highway Code reproduction, or a hazard-perception simulator. iOS work is outside this phase. Preparing a paid MVP does not mean that independent content approval or publication has occurred.

Lessons contain learning objectives, explanatory sections, an applied road scenario, a recap and traceable primary sources. Reading material must explain a principle before testing it; presenting a question explanation as a lesson is insufficient. Lesson body length is checked on explanatory paragraphs plus the scenario, without counting headings, source lists or repeated objectives. The intended range is 250–450 words per lesson.

## Legal, factual and media boundaries

- Preserve the Highway Code distinction between **MUST/MUST NOT** legal requirements and **should** advice. Do not convert an advisory following gap, recommended rest interval or pedestrian-junction provision into an unsupported legal absolute.
- The current street-lit default-limit distinction is generally 30 mph in England/Scotland and 20 mph in Wales, unless signed otherwise. Vehicle class, towing, local signs and conditions still matter. A national-limit sign is not one universal speed.
- Towing lessons instruct checking current entitlement and manufacturer limits. Do not revive obsolete licence-date/weight shorthand as a universal rule.
- Emergency-assistance material is bounded revision, not practical first-aid training. It emphasises scene safety, accurate emergency calls, reassurance, and not moving injured people or removing helmets unnecessarily. It does not certify competence or replace emergency-dispatcher guidance.
- Use only existing curated illustrations and the registered original videos where relevant. A retained image in the 121-draft gallery is not permission to use it for teaching. See `ASSET_LICENCES.md` and `IMAGE_LIBRARY_REVIEW.md`.
- Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except identified exclusions. Preserve DfT Crown-copyright attribution for government sign artwork. No protected DVSA question-bank wording, competitor content or manufacturer artwork is copied into this course.
