## Editorial verification before commercial publication

- Cross-check factual rule citations after Highway Code updates.
- Use "MUST" only for legal requirements and "should" for advisory rules.
- Second-review every question for ambiguous distractors and more than one defensible answer.
- Record revision date, reviewer and corrections in a future content management process.
- These questions currently cover Great Britain; DVA Northern Ireland exam differences need separate review.
