## Appropriate placement

The 20 new question pictures are individual controls, vehicle components or dashboard symbols. Their captions and answer labels stay hidden before answering. Accessibility descriptions describe visible shapes rather than naming a fault or the correct response.

Annotated crossing and box-junction diagrams, and roadside examples that supplement existing sign questions, are shown after answering or revealing a flashcard. The original quiz diagram remains visible before answering. No assets are attached solely by topic or a suggested question ID.

Two previously selected drawings are held from teaching: the red-X motorway scene does not clearly associate the closure with a lane, and the rear-fog drawing lacks an unambiguous standard fog-light treatment. Both remain available in the full review collection. Other unselected images include variants, subjects not yet matched and scenes requiring further checks; they are not all declared defective.

The existing `gb-signs-002` stem describes the car/motorcycle order differently from the drawing, so that question is not assigned the new photograph. The photograph is used in the accurate no-motor-vehicles sign lesson instead. The original question record remains preserved for independent editorial review. The zebra-priority annotation is likewise not attached to the separate zig-zag parking/overtaking question merely because both concern crossings.

## Sources checked

The scenarios and distractors are newly written. Government guidance supplies the underlying rules; manufacturer guidance supports common dashboard symbol meanings. Particular vehicles can differ, so the question explanations direct learners to their vehicle handbook.

- [DVSA vehicle-safety guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/car-show-me-tell-me-vehicle-safety-questions/car-show-me-tell-me-vehicle-safety-questions): oil and hydraulic brake-fluid checks.
- [Highway Code Annex 6](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/annex-6-vehicle-maintenance-safety-and-security): warning displays, clear vision, mirrors and luggage.
- [Highway Code rules 89–102](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/rules-for-drivers-and-motorcyclists-89-to-102): seat belts and child restraints.
- [DfT regulatory signs](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/know-your-traffic-signs/regulatory-signs) and [warning signs](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/know-your-traffic-signs/warning-signs): meanings of the existing sign questions and matching examples.
- [Ford dashboard guide](https://www.ford.co.uk/support/how-tos/more-vehicle-topics/lights-and-bulbs/what-do-the-lights-on-my-dashboard-mean) and [Ford warning-lamp reference](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/Ford_Content/vdirsnet/OwnerManual/Home/Content?ProcUid=G1298234&Uid=G1276543&buildtype=web&countryCode=USA&div=f&languageCode=en&moidRef=G539493&userMarket=GBR&vFilteringEnabled=False&variantid=2263): indicator meanings and the distinction between a start-up check, persistent warning, and an active feature. No manufacturer artwork was copied.
