Reviewed the repository on `main` including application UI, question data, SQLite storage, domain tests, build workflow, and documentation. Cross-checked release stack and actual test rules with the latest official Expo, React Native, GOV.UK, DVSA, and GitHub guidance.

No successful Android APK or iOS simulator job, device exercise, npm dependency install, or end-to-end test is confirmed at the time of writing. Lack of an observable status is **not** evidence of success or failure. Do not publish until verification completes.

## Confirmed official exam facts — Great Britain, car

- 50 multiple-choice questions, 57-minute time limit, 43 correct to pass.
- 3 questions relate to one short video case study.
- Hazard perception has 14 clips with 15 scored developing hazards and a 44/75 pass mark.
- Official test includes flags and reviewing unanswered questions, plus a break of up to three minutes before the hazard section.

Sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/multiple-choice-questions
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/pass-mark-and-result
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/hazard-perception-test

**Scope note:** GOV.UK car theory test guidance in this audit applies to Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales). Northern Ireland uses a separate agency and needs its own editorial verification before advertising a comprehensive UK-wide simulator.

## Intellectual property and branding

- Keep independently authored questions and original hazard scenes instead of copying DVSA's revision questions or clips.
- DVSA requires a copyright licence to reproduce its bank or official hazard-perception clips.
- Highway Code material and government road-sign images are available subject to the Open Government Licence and attribution/accuracy requirements.
- Avoid official DVSA branding, logos or claims of endorsement.
- Review potential naming confusion with existing official products.

Sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/make-and-sell-driving-theory-test-revision-products
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/traffic-sign-images

## CI acceptance gates to require

1. Fresh `npm ci` from a committed lockfile.
2. SDK compatibility diagnostic and strict TypeScript validation.
3. Unit tests for question integrity, exam scoring, persistence and navigation.
4. Successful Expo exports for both platforms.
5. Successful installable Android APK and iOS simulator app artifacts.
6. Automated UI acceptance: answer question, view explanation, save bookmark, quit/reopen, switch airplane mode, resume, complete timed mock and verify score.
7. Manual editorial sign-off for each question's answer and rule citation.
8. Offline content and video playback checks on a real device.
9. Store policy, artwork and revenue model approval before release.
