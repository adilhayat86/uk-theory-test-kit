Driving Theory Test UK: Learn

Privacy notice

Last revised: 18 November 2026

Your learning stays on your phone.

Study without an account, advertising or tracking. The Android app stores your learning locally and does not send your study history to a developer server.

What the app saves

The app keeps the information needed to continue and review your study:

  • Lesson reading positions and your explicit study confirmations.
  • Question answers, topic-check results and revision progress.
  • Saved questions and road-sign notes.
  • Practice and mock attempts, including answers, flags, timing, results and the study confirmation attached to a mock.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not provide cloud backup, account sync or transfer of progress to another phone. Its Android backup configuration opts out of automatic cloud backup and device transfer.

What you do not need to provide

You do not need to give the app your name, email address, password, location, contacts, photographs, government ID or payment-card details to study. The app does not include advertising, analytics or a remote learner-profile service. We do not sell your study history.

Offline content and external links

Lessons, questions, illustrations and the original case-study animations are included with the app and work offline. Optional source and help links open another app or your browser and need a connection. The destination service handles information under its own policies; your study history is not automatically attached.

Purchases and technical diagnostics

The app is intended as a one-time paid Google Play download with all included study content available. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Google Play processes the purchase and handles payment and account information under Google's privacy policy. Android and Google Play may separately process store or technical diagnostics under their settings and terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your study data remains until you reset it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The in-app reset control removes local learning progress, saved items and sessions. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → Driving Theory Test UK: Learn → Storage → Clear data. There is no remote learner account to delete.

Resetting, uninstalling or replacing your phone can permanently remove your learning history. The app has no cloud copy from which to restore it. Copies made independently using device-management or manual tools are outside the app's control.

Support and privacy questions

Email adilhayatkamker@gmail.com. If you choose to email, the support mailbox receives your sender address and the information you include. The app does not automatically attach study history, device logs or other personal details. Include only what is needed to explain your question. You may ask about deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

About this app

This is independent revision material for Great Britain car learners. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by DVSA, and it does not cover Northern Ireland-specific rules. It does not replace current official road rules or practical driving instruction.

Changes to this notice

The revision date will change if the app's data handling changes. Any future account, backup, analytics or other data feature would need an updated notice before that feature is released.
