## Store text

**App name:** Driving Theory Test UK: Learn

**Short description:** Learn, practise and track GB car theory progress. Offline. One purchase.

**Full description:**

Build understanding before testing yourself with independent Great Britain car-theory revision. Read clear lessons, check each topic, revise mistakes and track your progress toward a multiple-choice mock.

Everything is included in one purchase. No adverts, subscriptions or account are needed inside the app. Lessons, illustrations, questions and original video scenarios work offline after installation.

- Learn through 38 lessons across 15 topics, with road examples, recaps and source references.
- Check understanding with ten-question topic checks. Your reading and test results are tracked separately.
- Practise 309 original revision questions, revisit mistakes and save questions for later.
- Study 30 illustrated road-sign notes and try the recognition quiz.
- Take a 50-question, 57-minute multiple-choice mock with three questions about one of three original silent video case studies.
- Enter a mock directly after confirming that you have studied here or elsewhere.
- Review wrong and unanswered questions, follow links back to lessons and continue saved work after restarting.

**Scope:** This is independent Great Britain car-theory revision. It is not affiliated with DVSA and does not contain the licensed official question bank or a complete Highway Code reproduction. Hazard-perception testing and Northern Ireland-specific material are not included. The videos are original educational animations, not official DVSA footage. Practice scores do not guarantee an official test pass.

**Your data:** Learning progress and saved items stay in this app on your phone. There is no account or remote progress server. Automatic app-data cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Uninstalling or resetting the app removes that local history. External source links need an internet connection.
